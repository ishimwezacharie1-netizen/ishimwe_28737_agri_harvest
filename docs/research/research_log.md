## Dataset inspection and quality profile

**File:** `data/raw/adm_crop_data_raw_RW.csv`  
**Download:** [HarvestStat Africa dataset on HDX](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/harveststat-africa)  
**Extract:** Rwanda country file from `fdw_raw_data_v1.0.zip`.

- **Rows and values:** 13,725 observations; 9,123 nonblank values; 4,602 missing values (33.5%). The CSV has an unnamed first column that must be handled in staging.
- **Dimensions:** 38 `product` names, 42 distinct `geographic_unit_name` labels, three indicators (`Area Planted`, `Quantity Produced`, `Yield`), and historical season-year labels.
- **Units:** the extract mixes `ha`, `t`, and `MT/ha`; validate indicator-unit combinations before analysis or import.
- **Missing by decade, all indicators:** 1980s: 214/645 missing (33.2%); 2000s: 4,330/7,383 (58.6%); 2010s: 58/5,697 (1.0%). This disparity should be visible in dashboard filters and caveats.
- **Yield completeness by crop:** yams 170/221 (76.9%); mixed beans 109/151 (72.2%); Irish potato 202/285 (70.9%); cassava 199/281 (70.8%); white maize 181/262 (69.1%). These are nonmissing-row rates only, not claims of continuous annual series.
- **Crop-by-decade examples (all indicators):** mixed beans have 66.7% missing in 1980s (20/60 populated), 29.9% in 2000s (103/147), and 0% in 2010s (246/246); Irish potato has 3.3% in 1980s (58/60 populated), 50.2% in 2000s (163/327), and 0% in 2010s (468/468); white maize has 50.0% missing in 2000s (162/324) and 0% in 2010s (462/462). Missingness is not evenly distributed by crop, period, or indicator.
- Example source rows, suitable for validating import mapping: Bugesera / white maize / Season A 2017 / Yield / 0.83372726 / MT/ha; Gatsibo / Irish potato / Season A 2017 / Yield / 3.20545455 / MT/ha.

## Evidence and scope notes

- The real-data benchmark is the project core. `AGG_CROP_STATISTIC` and its source, crop, season, indicator, and area dimensions will be populated from the verified extract.
- There are no farmer, cooperative, farm, or plot identifiers in this dataset. Farm-level entities are a future, separate extension requiring a consented, real source; never fabricate linkage to aggregate observations.
- **Citation check:** an exact Koide et al. (2025) farm-record-keeping reference could not be verified from the author/year/topic lookup. It is not cited. Khusaini and Yusuf (2025) is a verified, related digital-record-keeping review; if Koide was intended, add its full title or DOI before replacing this source.
- HDX lists HarvestStat Africa under CC0 1.0; cite the source and preserve the unmodified raw extract. The secondary World Bank dataset is listed under CC BY and requires attribution.
