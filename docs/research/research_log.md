## Dataset inspection and quality profile

**File:** `data/raw/adm_crop_data_raw_RW.csv`
**Download:** [HarvestStat Africa dataset on HDX](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/harveststat-africa)
**Extract:** Rwanda country file from `fdw_raw_data_v1.0.zip`.

- **Rows and values:** 13,725 observations; 9,123 nonblank values; 4,602 missing values (33.5%). The CSV has an unnamed first column that must be handled in staging.
- **Dimensions:** 38 `product` names, 42 distinct `geographic_unit_name` labels, and three indicators (`Area Planted`, `Quantity Produced`, `Yield`).
- **Temporal coverage:** only eight distinct year labels are present: **1984, 2006, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2012, 2013, and 2017**. There is no 1990s coverage and only one year in the 1980s. Use the data for comparisons by crop, area, and season, not long-term trend claims.
- **Season labels:** the observed year labels include seasons A and B in multiple years and season C in 2013; preserve the source labels instead of assuming every year has the same seasons.
- **Units:** the extract mixes `ha`, `t`, and `MT/ha`; validate indicator-unit combinations before analysis or import.
- **Missing by decade, all indicators:** 1980s: 214/645 missing (33.2%); 2000s: 4,330/7,383 (58.6%); 2010s: 58/5,697 (1.0%). This disparity should be visible in dashboard filters and caveats.
- **Yield completeness by crop:** yams 170/221 (76.9%); mixed beans 109/151 (72.2%); Irish potato 202/285 (70.9%); cassava 199/281 (70.8%); white maize 181/262 (69.1%). These are nonmissing-row rates only, not claims of continuous annual series.
- **Crop-by-decade examples, all indicators:** mixed beans have 66.7% missing in 1980s (20/60 populated), 29.9% in 2000s (103/147), and 0% in 2010s (246/246); Irish potato has 3.3% missing in 1980s (58/60 populated), 50.2% in 2000s (163/327), and 0% in 2010s (468/468); white maize has 50.0% missing in 2000s (162/324) and 0% in 2010s (462/462). Missingness is not evenly distributed by crop, period, or indicator.
- Example source rows, suitable for validating import mapping: Bugesera / white maize / Season A 2017 / Yield / 0.83372726 / MT/ha; Gatsibo / Irish potato / Season A 2017 / Yield / 3.20545455 / MT/ha.

## Evidence and scope notes

- The real-data benchmark is the project core. `AGG_CROP_STATISTIC` and its source, crop, season, indicator, and area dimensions will be populated from the verified extract.
- District planners and cooperative advisers need consistent, quality-checked comparisons; missingness and incompatible units can mislead them if shown without caveats. The dashboard will pair comparisons with completeness and unit-quality indicators.
- The source has no farmer, cooperative, farm, or plot identifiers. Farm-level entities are a future, separate extension requiring a consented, real source; never fabricate linkage to aggregate observations.
- HDX identifies HarvestStat Consortium as the dataset organization and its dataset metadata lists the license as "Other." An earlier resource listing identified CC0 1.0, but the current resource API has no file-level license value; verify the exact file/resource license before redistribution and retain attribution.
- The secondary World Bank dataset is listed under CC BY; retain source attribution when redistributing it.
